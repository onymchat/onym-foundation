Where this stands — plain words

Exists today

  • Native iOS and Android clients, and one replaceable default deployment of message carriage, media storage, and group verification.
  • Public, technology-neutral contract documents for twelve seats.

Designed to guarantee

  • The courier cannot read the letter; the verifier never sees the member list; the name is never the identity.
  • These are contract obligations the documents fix — not yet independently verified properties of the running code.

Open and unaudited

  • The system is alpha-grade and has had no independent audit. Identity rotation, forward-secret messaging, and conformance fixtures are explicitly open work.
  • Banking and naming seats are proposals in review. Metadata-exposure assumptions are documented in the whitepaper's threat model.

03

User safety without a master switch

Encrypted does not mean lawless. A proposed moderation seat lets users report abuse — child sexual abuse material, credible violence, unsolicited pornography — to an independent authority they accepted when they joined, and lets apps keep banned devices out. The powers stay separate: the authority judges, the app enforces, and neither can read anyone's messages. The moderation proposal →

01 · REPORT

You disclose what you received

Reporting shares the specific offending message from your own device, with cryptographic proof of who sent it. Nothing else leaves your phone — nobody scans anything.

02 · CASE

A case, not a black box

The accused is notified, sees the evidence and the exact rule they agreed to at onboarding, and gets a response window before any decision.

03 · VERDICT

Signed, reasoned, expiring

Bans carry written reasons, published durations, and an appeal path — including for a device's next owner. An authority that goes silent means dismissal, never a freeze.

04 · ENFORCEMENT

The app executes, mechanically

A banned device stops working in apps that adopted the authority — marked via Apple DeviceCheck or Google Play device recall, so reinstalling doesn't reset it. The open protocol itself is untouched.

No scanning, no backdoors — evidence exists only when a recipient chooses to disclose it.

Judges never profit from convictions — authorities earn per case decided, never per ban.

Consent precedes the case — the rules you can be judged by are the rules you signed.

Where the Foundation stands

Outside the transaction ring. Arrows go out — to audits, grants, infrastructure, standards, recognition. Never into the message path or any seat's revenue.

The network's commons — independent audits, conformance test suites, reference infrastructure, grants to seat pioneers — need funding before the seat economy can pay for itself. Founding sponsors make that possible, and are remembered for it.

Grants

Endowment

Only gifts expressly designated as permanent are locked long-term — ordinary founding sponsorship funds current work, so the commons outlives any single donor without starving its first years.
