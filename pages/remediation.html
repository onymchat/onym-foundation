02

Foundation-owned work

Governance and accountability this institution must supply rather than delegate to protocol prose.

Constitutional consistency

Generate numeric claims from one machine-readable policy; publish document precedence; fail closed on conflicts.

All nine directors

Give the sponsor-class, Ecosystem, and Public-Interest seats selection routes that no contribution can reach, plus a time-bounded founding transition. The sponsor-class route is undecided and bracketed.

Money and custody

Publish the founding budget, custody matrix, investment policy, gift terms, conflicts, complaints, and reports before conversion.

Concentration

Disclose common control and material infrastructure in grants, procurement, catalogs, and Foundation-funded defaults.

Incident ownership

Every grant and service agreement names an incident coordinator and one complaint path. Modularity cannot become a blame maze.

Legal adoption

The articles of the sihtasutus and the adopted policies—not this website—must lawfully implement the proposal.

03

Protocol-owned work

The Foundation can fund and publish this work, but cannot pretend a website implements it.

One vocabulary

Canonical seat types, profile IDs, schema IDs, manifest envelopes, signatures, and revocation.

Identity continuity

Descriptor, capabilities, seat keys, rotation, revocation, recovery, and secure operator succession.

Exit tests

Full state export, third-party conformance, provider replacement, and governed notary migration.

Commercial constitution

Canonical offers and entitlements plus real billing-broker insolvency, complaint, audit, and provider-exit duties.

Route accountability

A named assembler or incident coordinator for composed failures, without giving it identity sovereignty.

Draft repair

Resolve the missing bank dependency, notary replacement conflict, and Foundation constants in `onym-system`.

04

Proof before claims

The test is whether the original operators can disappear without taking identity, relationships, or state with them.

  1. Import a non-default manifest and remove the default Discovery catalog.
  2. Use independently authored UI, relay, blob, read, and submission components.
  3. Export all local state and migrate a group under its actual governance rule.
  4. Rotate compromised operator and identity keys without silent rollback.
  5. Publish every failure, data loss, switching cost, control-group overlap, and responsible incident owner.

Until this drill passes, the honest description is “blueprint for replaceability,” not “decentralized institution.”

05

Activation gates

Any failed gate keeps the Foundation non-operational.

  • Upstream and local money/vote rules agree
  • The articles implement both organs, all three nõukogu classes, and the transition
  • Budget, custody, gift, investment, conflict, privacy, incident, complaint, and retention policies published
  • Legal entity, lawful board, custody, and signature authority exist
  • Every pledge is re-consented against the final terms
  • Control-group and material-infrastructure concentration disclosed
