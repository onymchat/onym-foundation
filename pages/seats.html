THE SEAT MAP

Four layers, fifteen open roles.

The person in the middle. Distance is intimacy: protocol components touch the daily route; market services attribute trust; applications serve particular domains; legal institutions remain governed by law. The Foundation sits outside the route, funding common infrastructure. Registering interest is a hello, not a contract.

Protocol kernel

Identity, interface, message, blob, and notary boundaries. Replacement requires wire compatibility and, for shared state, governed migration.

Service market

Recovery, Discovery, audit, and arbitration. Signatures attribute claims; they do not manufacture competence or truth.

Application profiles

Charity, banking, distribution, and acquisition. Portability does not override regulation, custody, or domain liability.

Organizational governance

Sponsorship and recruitment. Signed records support accountability but do not replace legal appointment, labor law, or fiduciary duty.

Foundation support may include grant consideration, audit funding, conformance support, or introductions. Each has separate eligibility, conflicts, evidence, and legal terms; support is not protocol permission or a universal offer. Competitors are welcome where a reference operator already exists.

The Onym Foundation takes no commission, runs no user-facing seat for profit, and can be routed around by design.

Status tags are factual: "draft spec" means a public contract document exists and is marked draft; "in review" means the proposal is not yet merged; "reference operator exists" means one replaceable default deployment runs today. Nothing here is audited yet.
